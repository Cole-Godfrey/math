Named structures such as `Category`, `MeasurableSpace`, and `HasRankNullity` express mathematical assumptions through their fields and theorem binders. They do not add foundational axioms. Each declaration's actual use of `propext`, `Quot.sound`, and `Classical.choice` is recomputed from its full dependency graph.

Use `show NAME --proof`, `hierarchy NAME --format json`, or the browser explorer to inspect the exact formal type, proof, dependencies, and retained source. Upstream author notices and licenses remain in the index.

`tests/ComputabilityStatements.lean` checks the stated halting, Rice, Ackermann, degree, and simulation signatures against the registered declarations. Computation subjects separate recursive functions and oracle reductions from machine models and execution semantics.

`tests/AlgebraStatements.lean` checks the Galois correspondences, primitive element and normal basis statements, profinite limit, radical solvability implication, and Nullstellensatz signatures. Their field, separability, finite dimension, closed subgroup, and algebraic closure assumptions appear explicitly in these checks.

`tests/NumberTheoryStatements.lean` checks reciprocity, Pell solutions, ideal factorization, class numbers, the Minkowski bound, unit rank and decomposition, regulator positivity, the product formula, and prime splitting. Prime, parity, nonzero, number field, and conductor-exponent hypotheses remain explicit.

`tests/ProbabilityStatements.lean` checks conditional expectations, both Borel-Cantelli lemmas, the zero-one law, the Banach-valued strong laws, the real central limit theorem, Gaussian moments, optional stopping and sampling, and martingale convergence. The tests distinguish pairwise from mutual independence, almost everywhere from Lp and distributional convergence, and L1 boundedness from uniform integrability.
